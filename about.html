About me

Who I am

I'm a business operator and brewer based in Columbus, Ohio. I've spent my career turning plans into working businesses: setting up the legal entity, raising the capital, managing the build-out, getting the permits, and then running the day-to-day operation.

Most recently I've been managing operations and fundraising at Rockmill Brewery. Before that I owned and ran Buzzsaw Brewing, a commercial brewery I took from corporate strategy through to opening and operation. I enjoy the mix of detail and big picture that work demands, from quality control on a single batch to an investor presentation.

Beyond work

Before business, my focus was swimming. At Denison University I was a 13-time All-American and an NCAA champion in 2011, and in 2012 I qualified for the Olympic Trials. The discipline of training, tracking progress, and showing up every day carries over into how I work.
