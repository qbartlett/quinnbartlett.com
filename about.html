About me

I'm Quinn, a brewer and business operator based in Columbus, Ohio. I like building things that work: a brewery, a business, a plan that holds up once real people and real deadlines get involved.

I'm happiest when I'm covering both ends of a business, from getting one batch exactly right to putting together an investor presentation.

How I got here

  1. In the water

    Swimming came first. At Denison University I was a 13-time All-American, won an NCAA title in 2011, and qualified for the 2012 Olympic Trials, while studying athletic training.

  2. Brewing and building

    In 2021 I started Buzzsaw Brewing and took it from corporate strategy through fundraising, construction, and licensing to an operating brewery. I later brewed at Hoster Brewing, working across 1, 5, and 15 BBL systems.

  3. Today

    At Rockmill Brewery I combine brewing with business management: production, compliance, and fundraising.

Beyond work

Swimming gave me habits I still rely on: training consistently, tracking progress, and showing up every day.
